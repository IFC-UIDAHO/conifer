01 · INPUT

What you bring

A tree list — stand, plot, DBH. Stand-level LiDAR or spectral metrics give the model something to borrow from. Stand polygons turn results into maps.

02 · METHOD

What it does

Bins DBH, works out the sampling covariance for your plot design, fits the small-area model, then calibrates intervals against your own held-out plots.

03 · OUTPUT

What you get

Stand and stock tables, per-acre summaries, QMD and basal area, maps of structure and uncertainty, an Excel workbook and a printable report.
